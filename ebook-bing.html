Text Types 5 jenis
Jenis teks yang diujikan dalam TKA Bahasa Inggris
01
Descriptive

Describing People, Places, Things

Uses specific nouns & adjectives, present tense, sensory details.

Example: "Bali is a beautiful island with stunning landscapes and rich culture."
02
Recount

Retelling Past Events

Uses past tense, time connectives (then, after that, finally).

Example: "Last summer, I had the opportunity to work as an intern at a local sports club."
03
Narrative

Stories with Conflict & Resolution

Has characters, setting, plot, and often a moral lesson.

Example: "The lion learned that even small creatures can do great things."
04
Procedure

How to Do or Make Something

Uses imperative verbs, time connectives (first, then, finally).

Example: "First, wash all the fruits carefully. Then peel the banana."
05
Analytical Exposition

Presenting Arguments & Persuading

Uses present tense, modals (should, must, can), thesis → arguments → reiteration.

Example: "I strongly believe that social media harms teen mental health."

Competency Levels 3 level
Pemahaman Tekstual · Inferensial · Evaluasi & Apresiasi
1
Pemahaman Tekstual

What it tests: Finding explicit information directly stated in the text.

Key Skills: Identifying main ideas, finding details, understanding vocabulary in context.

Question Pattern: "What does the text say about...?" · "According to the passage..."
2
Pemahaman Inferensial

What it tests: Understanding implied information and drawing conclusions.

Key Skills: Making predictions, drawing conclusions, identifying cause-effect.

Question Pattern: "What can be inferred...?" · "What will most likely happen...?"
3
Evaluasi & Apresiasi

What it tests: Making judgments and responding to texts emotionally and aesthetically.

Key Skills: Evaluating arguments, identifying author's purpose, responding to moral lessons.

Question Pattern: "What is the main lesson...?" · "Which statements support the argument...?"
Facts vs Opinions analisis
Membedakan fakta dan opini dalam teks
Facts

Can be verified · based on evidence · objective

Example: "The sun rises in the east." · "Indonesia has more than 17,000 islands."
Opinions

Subjective · based on belief/feeling · cannot be proven

Example: "Social media is harmful to teenagers." · "Bali is the most beautiful island."

Cause and Effect sebab-akibat
Hubungan sebab-akibat dalam teks
Signal Words

Cause: because, since, as, due to

Effect: therefore, so, consequently, thus, hence, as a result

Example
Cause: Thuy Tinh lost the competition.
Effect: He attacked with floods.
Compare and Contrast persamaan & perbedaan
Membandingkan informasi dalam teks
Compare (Similarities)

Show how things are alike

Signal words: both, also, similarly, likewise

Example: Both Son Tinh and Thuy Tinh love the princess.
Contrast (Differences)

Show how things are different

Signal words: but, however, unlike, although

Example: Son Tinh is calm, but Thuy Tinh is angry.
Tips for TKA English strategi
Before Reading

Preview the questions – know what to look for

Identify the text type – predict the structure

Note the competency – explicit or implicit information?

During Reading

Read actively – underline key points

Pay attention to transitions – however, therefore, moreover

Notice the author's tone – persuasive? informative? entertaining?

Answering Questions

Eliminate wrong answers – remove obviously incorrect options

Return to the text – find evidence for your answer

For multiple answers – check each option individually

Don't overthink – the answer is usually supported by the text
